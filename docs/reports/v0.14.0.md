## Design notes

- The lookup is the `--blind-spot` flag of `me`, not part of plain `me`, so the plain output (and
  its snapshots) stay offline and unchanged.
- The official list is read from its cache only; the lookup never fetches the marketplace.
- Official picks are a short list on purpose (security: `security-guidance`, `/security-review`;
  refactoring: `/simplify`). Other topics are everyday work, so they need a strong catalog match
  or the answer is "nothing needed".
- A repo the scan flags high, a heavy setup and a dismissed repo are never offered. Offline, no
  scan runs.
- `kickoff.mjs` now exports `goodEnough`, `needProfile` and `flaggedHigh` (no behavior change).
