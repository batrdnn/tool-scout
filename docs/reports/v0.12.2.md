## Found by the reviews

- The security-guidance review of the M5 commit: a hand list of invisible characters misses
  variation selectors, other format characters and combining marks. Fixed in `fc9b6f8` by
  dropping the Unicode classes. The look-alike table is still hand-written, not the full TR39
  confusables set (no runtime dependencies); the review doc says so.
- The review of the M6 commit: `lstat` then `chmod` can follow a symlink swapped in between.
  Once the folder is 0700 no other user can reach inside it, so this wasn't reachable; `dfb287b`
  closes it anyway, and also stops a `FIFO` from blocking the walk.
