The personas are in the order quant-ios, webdev, platform. Script p@25 is 0.51 (main) and 0.15
(holdout), and recall@25 is 1.00 on both.

What the table says:
- **The fixed denominator removes the "cautious judge" bonus.** v0.4's holdout 0.67 was really
  0.53 under fp@5.
- **Item 3 got the pick count to 3–4, but at a cost on the holdout.** Forced to show 3, the
  judge filled the platform persona with generic tools the labeler rejected (KubeSphere, a
  code-review graph). That pushed holdout fp@5 from 0.53 to 0.40. fp@3 is back at 0.56. Main
  improved to fp@3 1.00 and fp@5 0.67.
- **Each cell is a single judge sample on a small set.** One pick moves a persona's fp@5 by 0.2.

**quant-ios before and after item 4:**
- **Profile:** its manifest has pandas, numpy, matplotlib, jupyter and pytest, and **no finance
  library**. So weights went from data 3.5 / testing 9.2 / finance 2.7 to data **17.5** /
  testing **17.2** / finance 2.7.
- **Script:** the main-set top 5 went from Playwright, mobile-mcp ✔, MobileBuildMCP ✔,
  Vibe-Trading ✔, terraform-skill to Playwright, terraform-skill, bama ✔, Vibe-Trading ✔,
  day-trader ✔. Script p@3 fell from 0.67 to 0.33.
- **End to end:** main went from 1.00 / 0.60 to 1.00 / 0.60 (fp@3 / fp@5), with picks
  MobileBuildMCP, mobile-mcp and tradingview-mcp, all relevant. Holdout stays 0.33 / 0.20.
- **Caveat:** a library applies to every session in its project, so broad libraries (pandas,
  pytest) inflate broad topics. Finance would only gain for a project that declares a finance
  library.

## Start testers?

**Yes**, with a small group (5–10) whose histories have 20+ sessions of real work. Reasons:
- The blocking issue (vendor-SDK false picks at #1) is fixed and has regression tests.
- What the skill shows now holds about 1.7 relevant picks out of 3 on the blind holdout
  (fp@3 0.56).
- Further offline tuning is limited by small, model-labeled sets.

Testers' `stats --share` useful rate is the number to watch. If it comes in below about 40%,
the next step is better ranking signals, not more SKILL.md wording.
